### 🚀 About Me

I am a Computer Science student who believes in working smarter, not harder. While I study traditional software development and build full-stack applications for my university courses, my true passion and professional focus lie in **AI Automation**. 

Instead of writing thousands of lines of code to do simple tasks, I love leveraging platforms like **n8n** and **Zapier** alongside AI to eliminate repetitive work, optimize business workflows, and build systems that actually save people time.

### 💻 What I'm Doing Right Now

- ⚙️ **Currently Building:** **Gearshare** — My ongoing Software Engineering Lab group project.
- 🏠 **Past Work:** **Grihaloy** — A housing and flat management system I developed using Django.
- 🤖 **Professional Focus:** Designing done-for-you AI systems that automatically reply to leads and manage follow-ups for coaches and consultants.
